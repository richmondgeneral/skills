# Contributing

Richmond General is a **pre-release** project — at once a working private operation, a public portfolio (the build history *is* the point), and an eventual source of reusable pieces for other small Square retailers.

## Status: not accepting external contributions yet

This repository is **open to view and learn from** under the [Apache-2.0 license](./LICENSE), but **outside contributions (issues, pull requests, patches) are not being accepted until the project is released and stable** — which is still a way off.

- Please don't open PRs or issues for now — they won't be reviewed or merged.
- You're welcome to read, learn from, and (per Apache-2.0) reuse the code.
- The genuinely-reusable pieces (the best skills, possibly an open-source Square app) may later be extracted into their own clearly-contributable repos once stable — watch this space.

## Why

This is one operator's system, built in the open as a portfolio. A clean public/private boundary, a documented architecture, and stability come first; a contribution workflow comes later. Thanks for understanding.
