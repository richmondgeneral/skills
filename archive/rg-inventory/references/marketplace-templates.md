# Marketplace Listing Templates

Templates and guidelines for creating product listings across Square Online, eBay, Etsy, and other platforms.

## Title Formulas

### General Formula (80 characters max)
```
[Maker/Brand] [Pattern/Model] [Form] [Color/Material] [Size] [Era] [Condition]
```

**Priority order:**
1. Maker (Northwood, Fenton, Roseville) — collectors search by maker
2. Pattern name (Grape & Cable, Pinecone) — pattern collectors
3. Form (bowl, vase, pitcher) — functional search
4. Color/material — visual search, rarity signal
5. Size — practical buyers need this
6. Era — authentication signal
7. Condition flag only if exceptional ("Mint") or disclosure needed

**Examples:**
- `Northwood Grape & Cable Carnival Glass Bowl Amethyst 9" Ruffled Antique`
- `Roseville Pinecone Vase 121-7 Green Art Pottery 1930s Excellent`
- `Depression Glass Cherry Blossom Pitcher Pink Jeannette 1930s`

**Avoid:**
- ALL CAPS
- Excessive punctuation (!!!, ***)
- "Rare" unless genuinely rare
- "Antique" on items < 100 years
- "L@@K" style (dated, unprofessional)

## Description Structure

### Opening Hook (1-2 sentences)
State what it is with one compelling detail.

```
Stunning amethyst Grape & Cable bowl by Northwood, featuring exceptional 
electric purple iridescence and the sought-after pie crust edge.
```

### Identification Block
```
Maker: Northwood Glass Company
Pattern: Grape & Cable
Form: Bowl, ruffled/pie crust edge
Color: Amethyst with multicolor iridescence
Mark: "N" in circle on base
```

### Measurements
```
Dimensions: 9" diameter × 4" tall
Weight: [if relevant for silver/metal]
```

### Condition Paragraph
Honest, specific, uses standard terminology (see Condition Language below).

### History/Context (Optional, 1-2 sentences)
Adds value for collectors, helps SEO.

```
Northwood's Grape & Cable pattern, introduced around 1910, remains one of 
the most beloved carnival glass designs. The amethyst color was a standard 
production color but shows exceptional iridescence on this example.
```

### Closing/CTA
```
A beautiful example for any carnival glass collection. Ships carefully 
packed and insured. Questions welcome.
```

## Condition Language

### Standardized Terms

| Term | Meaning | Use When |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Mint** | Perfect, as-made condition | No wear, no flaws whatsoever |
| **Excellent** | Near-perfect, minimal wear | Very light wear consistent with age only |
| **Very Good** | Light wear, no damage | Minor wear, small imperfections |
| **Good** | Moderate wear, no significant damage | Obvious wear, minor flaws |
| **Fair** | Heavy wear or minor damage | Chips, cracks, repairs, heavy wear |
| **As-Is** | Damage present | Significant damage, sold for parts/repair |

### Describing Flaws (Be Specific)

**Glass:**
- Small chip: "Tiny flea bite to rim, 2mm"
- Chip: "Small chip to base edge, 1/4 inch"
- Crack: "Hairline crack from rim, 1 inch" (disclose length/location)
- Wear to iridescence: "Light wear to high points of pattern"
- Sick glass: "Some clouding/haze to interior"

**Pottery:**
- Crazing: "Fine crazing to glaze, stable" or "No crazing"
- Chip: "Glaze flake to rim, 1/4 inch"
- Hairline: "Tight hairline from rim, does not go through"
- Repair: "Professional repair to handle" (always disclose)
- Kiln kiss: "Minor kiln kiss to base, as-made"

**Furniture:**
- Wear: "Patina consistent with age" (positive framing)
- Scratches: "Light surface scratches, easily polished"
- Veneer issue: "Small veneer chip to edge"
- Refinished: "Appears to retain original finish" or "Professionally refinished"

## SEO & Searchability

**Keyword placement priority:**
1. Title (most weight)
2. First sentence of description
3. Item specifics/attributes (platform fields)
4. Throughout description naturally

**Include searchable synonyms:**
- "Carnival glass" AND "iridescent glass"
- "Depression glass" AND "1930s glass"
- "MCM" AND "Mid-Century Modern"
- "Pottery" AND "Ceramics"

**Avoid keyword stuffing:**
- Bad: `Carnival Glass Bowl Carnival Glass Amethyst Carnival Purple Carnival`
- Good: `Amethyst Carnival Glass Bowl with Grape Pattern - Purple Iridescent`

## Platform-Specific Notes

### Square Online (Richmond General)
- Use clear, descriptive titles
- SEO-optimized permalinks with keywords
- Page title formula: `[Era] [Item Type] - [Key Feature] | Richmond General`
- Meta description: Include era, maker, condition, end with "Richmond, VA" for local SEO

### eBay
- Use Item Specifics completely (improves search ranking)
- 80-character title limit
- Mobile-first: front-load key info
- Promoted listings: strong titles matter more

### Etsy
- Tags are crucial (13 available)
- First 40 characters of title most important
- "Vintage" category requires 20+ years old
- Story/context resonates with Etsy buyers
