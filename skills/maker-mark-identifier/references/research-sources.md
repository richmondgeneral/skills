# Research Sources for Maker's Mark Identification

## Online Databases by Category

### Ceramics & Pottery

| Resource | Coverage | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Kovels (kovels.com) | American & European | Subscription; extensive mark database |
| The Marks Project (themarksproject.org) | American art pottery | Free; photos of actual marks |
| Gotheborg (gotheborg.com) | Asian ceramics | Free; excellent for Chinese/Japanese |
| Replacements Ltd (replacements.com) | Dinnerware patterns | Free; good for tableware ID |

**Search strategy:** "[visible text] pottery mark" or "[symbol description] ceramics mark [country if known]"

### Furniture

| Resource | Coverage | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Furniture City History | Grand Rapids makers | Regional focus |
| FADA (Furniture Archive Database) | American furniture | Limited but growing |

**Furniture dating approach:** Marks less standardized. Focus on:
- Construction methods (dovetails, joinery)
- Hardware style and materials
- Wood species and finish
- Any stamps, labels, brands, or stencils

### Jewelry

| Resource | Coverage | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Langantiques Encyclopaedia | Costume jewelry | Maker histories |
| RAOV (Rhinestones and Old Vintage) | Costume jewelry marks | Collector community |
| 925-1000 | Fine jewelry hallmarks | Overlaps with silver |

**Common jewelry marks:**
- 925 = Sterling silver
- 750 = 18K gold
- 585 = 14K gold
- 375 = 9K gold (UK common)

### Glass

| Resource | Coverage | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Glass Encyclopaedia (glass-encyclopaedia.com) | European art glass | Free; maker histories |
| EAPG databases | Pressed glass 1850-1910 | Various collector sites |
| Fenton Art Glass Collectors | Fenton-specific | Mark variations by era |

**Note:** Most antique glass is unmarked. Identification relies on pattern, form, and manufacturing characteristics rather than marks.

**For carnival glass:** Use `carnival-glass-appraiser` skill instead—pattern-based identification.

## Research Tips

**When web search fails:**
1. Try image search with photo of actual mark
2. Search "[piece type] unmarked" to find attribution discussions
3. Check collector forums (active ID threads)
4. Look for pattern/shape identification first, then narrow to maker

**Authentication red flags:**
- Marks too crisp/perfect on "old" pieces
- Modern mark styles on antique forms
- "Made in [Country]" on pieces styled pre-1914
- Inconsistent wear (mark pristine, piece worn)

**When confidence is low:**
- Acknowledge uncertainty clearly
- Provide research path taken
- Suggest specialist consultation for valuable pieces
- Auction house specialists often provide free verbal opinions

## Country of Origin Dating

| Mark Text | Date Implication |
|-----------|------------------|
| No country | Pre-1891 (or domestic) |
| "England" / "France" / etc. | 1891+ (McKinley Tariff) |
| "Made in England" | 1914+ (common), 1921+ (required) |
| "Nippon" | 1891-1921 |
| "Japan" | 1921+ |
| "Occupied Japan" | 1945-1952 |
| "West Germany" | 1949-1990 |
| "Czechoslovakia" | 1918-1939, 1945-1992 |
